`Tabs` is now govuk-frontend-react's Tabs markup with tab behaviour from `@govuk-react/primitives`: tabs from tablet width up, and links to panels below it or without JavaScript. Select tabs with `defaultValue`, or `value` and `onValueChange`. `RadiosComposite`, or `RadiosRoot` with `RadioTab` and `RadioPanel`, switch tabs without JavaScript too. See MIGRATION.md.
