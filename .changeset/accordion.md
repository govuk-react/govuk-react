New `Accordion`: govuk-frontend-react's markup, with behaviour from `@govuk-react/primitives`. As in govuk-frontend, every section is shown without JavaScript. Once JavaScript runs, each heading is a button that shows and hides its section, with "Show all sections", find in page and remembered sections. Sections open with `defaultValue`, or `value` and `onValueChange`.
